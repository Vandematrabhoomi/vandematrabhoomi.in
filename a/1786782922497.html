Vande Matrabhoomi organised a debate competition

Ghaziabad: 1:52 p.m.- As part of the social outreach activities, today on the occasion of the 80th independence day of India, Vande Matrabhoomi, a digital media network, organised a debate competition at Gaur Green Vista Society, Nyaya Khand I, Indirapuram, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh. Ms. Aadhya Shree, student of Cambridge school, Indirapuram, was declared as the winner. For doing the honours Commodore (Retd.) , Indian Navy, Ashok Rai, AVSM was invited as the chief guest. Mr. Ashok Rai emphasised the role of GenZ and Gen Alpha in Nation building. He added that the experience and knowledge of the Gen X and senior citizens should be utilised for the development of the country and it is the duty of every citizen to give appropriate respect to the older generation and culture of the country. The program was a mellifluous confluence of fun, frolic and knowledge sharing. @aapka panna desk
